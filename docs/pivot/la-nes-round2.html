LA Freebies: round 2

Three things to pick from. Each option has a number, so just tell me "logo 4, teams B, nav 2" (or mix and match).

1. Logo: say "LA" and "sports"

All eight are drawn on a 32×32 pixel grid in NES colors, shown at App Store size and home-screen size. Purple-and-gold and blue-and-white are the closest NES colors to the Lakers and Dodgers.

Heads-up on team colors: each team owns its color scheme as part of its brand. An app icon pairing "LA" with Lakers purple-and-gold and Dodger blue can read as officially affiliated, and Apple or a team could object. Options tagged team colors carry that risk. Options tagged LA flag colors use the City of Los Angeles flag (green, gold, red), which belongs to no team. Not legal advice.

2. Teams: show all 14, keep it calm

Yes, show every LA team. Off-season teams go grey with their return month. Fourteen tiles at once gets busy, though, so here are four ways to organize them. All four keep what you like: tap a team to follow all its deals, ✓ on followed teams, and a partial "1/2" count.

3. Navigation that feels like an NES

Today the app uses the standard iOS tab bar with pixel icons. That's why it feels clunky: iOS chrome wearing an 8-bit costume. These replace the chrome itself. All four keep a one-tap way to every screen and work with VoiceOver.

My recommendation

  1. Logo 2 (palm sunset in LA purple, gold and blue) if you accept the team-color risk; logo 7 (palm on the LA flag) if you want zero risk.
  2. Teams A (season board) as the default, with B's ◀ ▶ sport pages as a filter once there are more teams with deals.
  3. Nav 1 (game HUD bar) plus 4 (title screen and wipes). Save the 3 map for Tonight once we have live game data.
