10 exits

Freebies, LA only

Baseball is over. Here are ten ways to rebuild the app around every LA team and the food deals they trigger, with what changes in each.
↑ Research: what's out there ↘ Ten options, each with a full walkthrough ↗ Recommended route

Prepared 15 Nov 2026 from three inputs: a read of this repo, web research on LA deals, competitors and data feeds, and guidance from the Expo, mobile-taste and apple-skills plugins (redesign, design-system, App Store keyword and description skills). Items marked unverified need a human check against the sponsor's own page before they ship.

What we found

Four facts shape every option below.

1. The competition is thin

Two small indie iOS apps cover SoCal sports deals. None of them push from live game data at the moment a deal fires.

2. Winter deals are scarce

Right now only a handful of LA deals can be claimed from home: Ducks + McDonald's for sure, Kings + El Pollo Loco probably, and LAFC + Ono during the playoffs. No current Lakers, Rams, Chargers or Galaxy trigger deal turned up.

3. Chick-fil-A moved in-venue

Since 2026, Chick-fil-A SoCal deals (Ducks, LAFC, Clippers) appear to be claimable only inside the arena. An app that tells someone at home about them wastes their attention. We need an "at the game only" flag.

4. Free data has strings attached

ESPN's unofficial API returns JSON for all eight leagues we need, but Disney's terms ban automated and commercial use. The NHL API is in the same position. Budget for a licensed feed before monetizing.

What the codebase allows today

Ready to use

  • Source interface with a league registry: adding a league means adding a package.
  • Server-driven UI: screens render blocks from /config, so layout changes can ship without an app release.
  • Feature flags per league, redemption windows (same_day, next_day, week), Expo push, reminders.
  • A richer rule evaluator (internal/rules) with compound conditions, already written but not used.

Blocks the pivot

  • Only MLB has a Source. Metrics are flat ints with MLB names.
  • No teams table; team data is copied onto every event row.
  • The checker runs once each morning against "yesterday". That doesn't fit live alerts or late West Coast games.
  • No concept of ties or draws (MLS, NWSL, NHL shootouts), and no in-venue flag.
  • Mobile league map uses uppercase keys while the DB stores lowercase, so the filter never matches.

Rules for every option: keep the bundle ID io.bitsugar.freebies and the App Store record, which preserves ratings and installs. Rename the display name, icon, colors, listing and screenshots in a normal update. Don't put team names or logos in the app name or icon (App Store guideline 5.2 and team trademarks). Naming teams in the description as a factual reference is normal practice. The codebase was read at commit 0f20817; main has since moved to Expo SDK 58 (#60), so re-check the mobile details on the branch.

EXIT1

Straight LA rebrand

Keep the product. Change who it's for, what it's called, and how it looks.

For: any LA fan who wants free food when their team wins
Effort: S–M
Ships in: 1–2 weeks

The smallest move that's still a real pivot. Retire the San Diego, Colorado and Arizona offers, add every LA team, seed the deals we can verify, and give the app an LA identity. Everything else on this page builds on top of this, so it's the foundation whichever direction you choose.

App

  • New palette in useTheme.tsx; remove the ~15 hardcoded hex values
  • Replace the emoji tab icons with one icon family (a mobile-taste anti-pattern)
  • New icon, splash, adaptive icon, and the missing notification-icon.png
  • Fix the league flag case bug; read the league list from the API

Backend

  • Migration: deactivate non-LA events; seed league rows for nba, nhl, nfl, mls, nwsl, wnba
  • Source for NHL first (in season, with confirmed deals), then NBA
  • Add in_venue_only and verified_at to events

Store and docs

  • New name, subtitle, keywords, description and screenshots
  • README, AGENTS/CLAUDE.md, privacy policy

Brand

  • LA dusk palette: jacaranda, sodium-lamp amber, Pacific blue-black
  • Avoid team colors so no single fan base claims the app

EXIT2

In season now

The home screen follows the LA sports calendar, so the app never looks empty.

For: fans of more than one LA team
Effort: S
Ships in: 1 week

The biggest risk after a pivot is opening the app in February and finding nothing to claim. This option puts "who plays next and what it unlocks" at the top: tonight's LA games, the deal tied to each, and a quiet "back in March" row for teams in their off-season. Because the screens are server-driven, the ordering can live in the API and change week to week with no app release.

App

  • New TonightBlock: LA games today with their deal lines
  • Off-season teams collapse into one row with the return date
  • Empty state that points somewhere instead of "No deals"

Backend

  • Schedule endpoint per league (scoreboard by date)
  • A teams table with season_start/season_end
  • /config orders blocks by what's in season

EXIT3

Morning-after digest

One push at 7am: everything you won last night, and how to claim it.

For: casual fans who won't watch every game
Effort: S
Ships in: 3–5 days

Many LA deals redeem the next day (McGriddles, Del Taco, Ono, Panda), and your scheduler already runs at 7 and 9am PT. Batching every triggered deal into one daily summary cuts notification fatigue, fits the current architecture with no live data, and feels like a morning ritual. It's also what the GitHub scraper does by email, which shows people want it.

Backend

  • Group triggered events per user into one notification
  • Digest body: "2 deals: McGriddles · Burrito"
  • Skip on days with nothing (no "no deals today" spam)

App

  • A "This morning" screen that opens from the push
  • Settings: digest vs instant, delivery time

EXIT4

Live trigger alerts

Push the moment the 5th goal goes in, not the next morning.

For: fans watching the game
Effort: L
Ships in: 3–5 weeks

No competitor does this, so it's the real moat. It needs in-game polling and play-by-play data. The Clippers free-throw deal and the LAFC first-half-goal deal can't be detected from final scores at all. It's also where the data licensing question matters most: polling every 30 seconds across six leagues is plainly "automated access".

Backend

  • A live poller: only LA games in progress, 20–60s interval, cached
  • Event-level metrics: goals by period, free throws by quarter
  • Switch to the existing internal/rules evaluator for compound rules
  • Dedupe per game_id + rule, as today

Infra and legal

  • The CronJob model doesn't fit; needs a long-running worker or a per-minute job during game windows
  • Price a licensed feed (Sportradar or similar) before launch

  2. Extend the interface. LiveSource with GetLiveGame(ctx, gameID) (*LiveState, error) returning period, clock and an event list.

  6. Safety. Hold a trigger until the play is official (reviews and overturned goals happen). Add a 60-second confirmation delay.
  7. Load test on a busy night: Lakers + Clippers + Kings + Ducks at once.

Build it as phase 2, after the rebrand proves people want LA deals. Settle the data license first.

EXIT5

At the game mode

Some deals only work in your seat. Show those to the people in the seats.

For: season-ticket holders and anyone attending
Effort: M
Ships in: 2 weeks

Chick-fil-A's 2026 change turned a big share of LA deals into in-venue claims. Rather than hiding them, make them a mode: when you're at Crypto.com Arena, Intuit Dome, Honda Center, BMO Stadium, SoFi, Dodger Stadium or Angel Stadium, the app lists exactly what can trigger tonight and pushes the moment it does. Everyone else never sees these deals.

App

  • "I'm at the game" toggle (manual first; geofence later)
  • A venue card: tonight's in-venue triggers, claim steps

Backend

  • in_venue_only flag plus a venue on teams
  • Filter in-venue deals out of everyone else's feed and pushes

EXIT10

Local sponsor marketplace

Let an Echo Park taqueria say "free taco if LAFC scores 3" in five minutes.

For: local LA restaurants (they pay) and fans (they eat)
Effort: XL
Ships in: 2–3 months

Big chains already run their own team deals. Local places can't afford a team sponsorship, but they can afford "$49/month for our deal in front of 5,000 LA fans". That fills the Lakers/Rams/Chargers gap with deals nobody else has, and it's a real revenue model. It's also a second product: sales, billing, a business dashboard, and liability (avoid team trademarks in sponsor copy).

New surfaces

  • Web dashboard for businesses: build a rule, set a cap, see redemptions
  • Stripe billing

Engine

  • The internal/rules evaluator becomes public-facing
  • Redemption codes per user, capped per business
